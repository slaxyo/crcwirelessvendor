Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 23, 2025

CRC Wireless (also known as MAP Wireless) values your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your information when you interact with us online or in person, including when making payments or signing up for installment plans.

Information We Collect

We collect the following types of personal information when you make a purchase, sign up for services, or interact with us:

  • Name, phone number, and email address
  • Bank account information (if paying via ACH)
  • Purchase history, balances, and payment schedule
  • Device details and repair-related information

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Process transactions and installment payments securely
  • Verify your identity for fraud prevention
  • Send payment reminders, receipts, and service updates
  • Maintain records of past orders and balances

Use of Stripe and Financial Connections

For secure payment processing, we partner with Stripe. When you link a bank account for ACH payments, Stripe may collect and store your financial data in accordance with their Privacy Policy. We never see or store your bank credentials.

Sharing of Data

We do not sell your personal information. We only share data with third-party services (like Stripe) strictly for processing payments or compliance with legal obligations.

Data Storage

Your data is stored securely in the United States. We take reasonable measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, disclosure, or misuse.

Your Rights

You may contact us at any time to:

  • Update or correct your personal information
  • Request deletion of your data (subject to legal obligations)
  • Request a copy of your stored information
