Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 23, 2025

At CRC Wireless (also known as MAP Wireless), your privacy and trust are important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your personal data when you use our services, make purchases, or enroll in installment payments.

What We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Your name, phone number, and email address

  • Payment history, balances, and invoice details

Why We Collect It

We collect your data for the following purposes:

  • To process secure payments, including ACH installment billing
  • To send payment reminders, invoices, and receipts
  • To track service history and manage balances
  • To ensure legal compliance and protect against fraud

Third-Party Providers

We partner with Stripe for payment processing. When you submit payment details, Stripe may store and process your data securely. For more details, see Stripe's Privacy Policy.

Data Security & Storage

Your data is stored and secured within the United States using industry-standard encryption and security protocols. We do not sell or share your personal information with unrelated third parties.

Your Rights
